The coolest creature

The Frog is awesome because it comes in many different colors and there are so many fun facts about them. Frogs use their eyes to push food down their throats. When they blink, their eyes pull down into their head


Frogs also do not drink water through their mouths. They absorb the water through a special patch of skin on their bellies and thighs. They also come in many different sizes. For example, the Goliath Frog grows up to 15 inches long and weighs up to 7 pounds. The smallest frog is less than half an inch long.
